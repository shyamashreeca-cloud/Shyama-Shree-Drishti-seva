HUM KAISE SAATH DE SAKTE HAIN

Support Ka Matlab Sirf Madad Nahi.

Support ka matlab hai kisi ko khud apni zindagi jeene ke liye confidence aur opportunities dena.

01

Family Guidance

Families ko visually impaired bachche ki needs, education aur daily support ko samajhne mein guidance.

02

Education Support

Braille, accessible learning aur technology-based learning opportunities ke liye support.

03

Confidence Building

Bachchon ko skills develop karne aur apni abilities par bharosa karne ke liye encouragement.

04

Independent Living

Daily-life skills aur mobility jaise areas mein age-appropriate learning support.

05

Technology

Accessible digital tools aur learning technology se opportunities ko expand karna.

06

Community

Aisa environment banana jahan bachcha accepted, respected aur included feel kare.
